About me

Connecting observations to the physics of galaxies.

I study how astronomical observations can be used to understand the physical systems that shape our universe.

Hello, I'm Christian Lambert.

I'm a graduate student in astrophysics at The University of Oklahoma.

My research focuses on galaxy dynamics and the use of observed gas and stellar motions to investigate galactic mass distributions and gravitational potentials.

I'm particularly interested in how dynamical models connect measurable kinematics to the underlying physics, and in how different observations and modeling assumptions affect the conclusions we can draw.

Research interests

  • Galaxy dynamics
  • Dark matter
  • Gravitational potentials
  • Gas kinematics
  • Stellar dynamics
  • Computational astrophysics
  • Scientific modeling
